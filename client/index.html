Questions

How much is delivery?

In the UK it's £1.99, and free on orders of £49 or more after discounts. Other countries have their own rates in local currency; see Shipping & returns.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Yes, to 13 countries. Tech, home and pantry items ship worldwide; fresh food (fruit, vegetables, dairy, eggs, meat, seafood and bakery) is UK-only. Choose your country with “Deliver to” at the top of any page to see prices in your currency.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, from Your orders, as long as it still says “Processing”. The items go straight back into stock.

What if something arrives damaged?

Fresh food can't be returned, but tell us within 48 hours through the contact page and we'll refund it.

Do I need an account?

You can browse, save a wishlist and fill your cart without one. You'll be asked to sign in when you check out.
