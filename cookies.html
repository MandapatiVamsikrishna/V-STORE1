Cookie notice

V-STORE doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. To make the shop work, it saves a few things in your browser's local storage:

  • vstore_cart, vstore_promo — your basket and promo code
  • vstore_wishlist — items you saved
  • vstore_orders, vstore_last_order — your order history and latest receipt
  • vstore_users, vstore_session — your account and whether you're signed in
  • vstore-country, theme — your delivery country and light/dark mode

These are essential for the shop to work, so there is nothing to opt into. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.
